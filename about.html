Our story · Kota, Rajasthan

Good eyewear should feel inevitable.

Mishra Optics began in 2016 with a small workbench, a box of Italian acetate and a stubborn belief: you shouldn't have to choose between a frame you love and a price you can live with. A decade later, we still make every frame in small batches — just with better tools and a few more pairs of hands.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise

Materials with a conscience

Italian acetate, plant-based bio-acetate and recycled metals. We choose materials that age beautifully and don't cost the earth to make.

Fit before fashion

A beautiful frame that pinches is a bad frame. Every silhouette is tested on real faces, in real light, for a full week before it's approved.

Priced honestly

No middlemen, no seasonal markups. We sell direct from our studio so you pay for the frame and the lenses — not the billboard.

Small-batch, since 2016
Inside the studio

Made slowly, on purpose.

Our Kota studio is equal parts workshop and showroom. Acetate sheets are cut, tumbled for three days, then polished by hand until the surface holds light the way we want it to. It takes longer. It also means we can check every frame before it leaves the bench.

  • Hand-polished, never rushedThree days of tumbling for a finish that stays glossy for years.
  • Checked by a humanEvery pair is inspected, adjusted and signed off in studio.
  • Adjustments for lifeWalk in for a free tune-up, whenever your frame needs one.

The long way round

A decade of small steps

We've grown the way we make frames: slowly, and with a lot of checking. Here are the moments that mattered.

2016The first workbench

Two brothers, one rented room in Kota and a first run of 40 round frames.

2018The Rangbari studio

We opened our doors to the public — and started free lifetime adjustments.

2021Our own lens lab

Bringing lens fitting in-house cut delivery times in half and waste by a third.

2024Jaipur, and beyond

A second studio in C Scheme, and our first 10,000 orders shipped pan-India.

2026The Monsoon Edit

Eight new silhouettes, our lightest frames yet, and a new bio-acetate range.
